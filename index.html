Independent Mobile App Development

Welcome to Eri Tech Studio. We design and build clean, purposeful, and modern utility applications for iOS and Android platforms.

Our Applications

  • Kohii — A dedicated coffee and espresso companion designed to log brew recipes, track extraction parameters, and dial in the perfect cup.
  • The Bake Log — A specialized mobile companion for sourdough bakers, designed to track hydration, fermentation times, and bake logs to perfect every loaf.
  • Warranty Box — A streamlined digital vault to store, track, and manage product warranties, receipts, and expiration alerts all in one secure place.
  • Cellar Book — A wine cellar companion that tracks drink windows on every bottle, with label photos, tasting notes on the 100-point scale, and an offline atlas of your collection's regions.
  • Leaflet — A plant care tracker with watering and feeding schedules, care reminders, and a photo timeline that shows a year of growth in one scroll.
  • The Travel Binder — A fully offline binder for flights, hotels, bookings, documents, packing and checklists, with the whole trip on one map. No account, no cloud.
