Our Apps

Six private logbooks for things worth keeping a record of. All fully offline. Premium your way: a subscription, or a one-time purchase that’s yours forever.

  • The Travel BinderFlights, hotels, bookings, documents, packing and a map of the whole trip. Works in aeroplane mode, in a country with no signal.
  • The Bake LogA baking journal for sourdough. Starter feedings that actually remind you, guided bakes, and a record of every loaf.
  • KohiiA brew journal for people who dial in. Espresso, pour-over, AeroPress, moka and cold brew, all logged offline.
  • Cellar BookWhat you own, what it is worth waiting for, and what is ready tonight.
  • LeafletWatering and feeding schedules that actually remind you, and a photo timeline showing a year of growth in one scroll.
  • Warranty BoxA warranty tracker app for iPhone and Android — every receipt, warranty and return deadline in one place, with a reminder before the window closes.
