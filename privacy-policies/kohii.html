Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-10-31

Data Collection

Kohii stores all your coffee data locally on your device. There is no account, no login and no server of ours, and we never receive your beans, brews, notes or photos — we cannot see, access, or recover them. The app can keep its own backup in your iCloud Drive or Google Drive, described under Backups to your own cloud below; that copy is yours, not ours. Apart from that backup, the only things that ever leave your device are anonymous crash diagnostics, anonymous usage analytics and, in the free version, the advertising data described under Ads below — none of them includes anything you have typed or photographed.

What Data Is Stored

Your bean bags, brew logs, ratings, and tasting notes are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. It leaves your phone only inside a backup: the automatic one to your own cloud, or a backup file you choose to save yourself.

Photos & Camera

If you add a photo to a bean or a brew, the app accesses your camera or photo library only at the moment you choose an image. Photos you select are copied into the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us; the only copy that leaves your device is inside your own backup.

Backups

Kohii lets you save your coffee log to a backup file and restore it later. The file is handed to the app or destination you choose through your device's share sheet; where it goes, and who can read it, is entirely under your control. We never receive, access, or transmit it.

Backups to your own cloud

Kohii backs up your journal automatically to your own cloud storage, so a new or reset device can bring it back. On iPhone and iPad the backup goes to the app's own folder in your iCloud Drive; it is not shown in the Files app. On Android it goes to a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, after you choose Back Up and allow it on Google's own screen; the app asks only for access to that hidden folder, which it alone can read, and never to your other files or to your Google account details. Each backup is a single file holding your whole journal — your coffees, bags, brews, ratings, notes and prices — with its photos. Each backup also carries a small summary — the date, the app version, your device's name, a random code that tells your devices apart, and how many coffees, brews and photos it holds — so the app can list your backups and show what is in them. The app keeps the three newest backups from each device and removes older ones. The backup travels from your device straight to Apple or Google; we never receive a copy, and it is stored under their privacy policies like your other files there. Restoring a backup replaces the journal on that device with the one in the backup. You can turn automatic backup off in Settings, which stops new backups. To delete the backups themselves, use iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Kohii, or Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Kohii → Delete hidden app data. Deleting the app does not delete these backups. On Android, the system's own device backup now holds only Kohii's journal database and its settings; your photos travel only inside the cloud backup above.

Crash reporting

Kohii uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash diagnostics (device model, OS version, and the technical state of the app at the moment of a crash). No personal information, coffee data, notes, or photos are ever transmitted. Crash reports exist solely so we can fix defects, and are covered under our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Usage analytics

Kohii uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features are used, so we can improve the app. It records anonymous, pseudonymous signals only — which screens you open, how often core features are used, whether Premium is active and when a subscription ends or resumes, the app version, your device model, and your coarse region (country level). It never records anything you type or photograph: not a coffee, a brew, a rating, a tasting note, a price, or a bag photo. Analytics is never used for advertising: advertising-personalisation signals are switched off. In Kohii 1.1 and earlier, in regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), analytics collection follows the choices you make in the ad consent form (Google Consent Mode).

Ads

From version 1.3.67, the free version shows a single banner advertisement. It appears at the foot of Today, the Shelf and the Journal, and on the coffee and brew pages you open from them — never while you brew, never in Settings, and never as a full-screen ad. Versions 1.2.62 to 1.3.66 show no advertisements and contain no advertising software. Versions 1.1 and earlier show a banner served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers to serve ads, after asking for consent where the law requires it. Ads are served through Appodeal, an advertising mediation platform, and the ad networks it works with (including AppLovin, BidMachine, Digital Turbine, InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff, Mintegral, myTarget and Unity Ads). To choose, show and measure ads, they may collect your device's advertising identifier, your coarse location (derived from your IP address), your device type and operating system, and which ads you see and tap. None of your coffees, brews, notes or photos is ever shared with them. Before any ad is requested, you are asked for your consent choices where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking prompt then asks whether ads may use your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline either, ads are still shown, but they are not personalised. You can change your consent choices at any time from Settings → Manage Ad Consent, and your tracking choice in your device's Settings. Appodeal's own policy is at https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy. Kohii Premium — the one-time unlock or the monthly subscription — removes advertising entirely: the ad software is not started at all.

In-App Purchases

Kohii Premium — a one-time unlock or a monthly subscription — is processed by the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We never see or store your payment details. The app asks your app store whether your purchase or subscription is active so it can unlock Premium. A subscription renews each month until you cancel it, which you do from your App Store or Google Play account settings; refunds are handled by the store.

Your Rights

All your data is yours. You can export it anytime from within the app, or delete it entirely by uninstalling the app. We have no central record of your data.
