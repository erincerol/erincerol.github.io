Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-08-18

Data Collection

Your plant journal is fully offline and stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store it on any servers, and there is no account, login, or cloud sync. The one exception is advertising on the free tier, which is handled by Google AdMob and described under Ads below; Premium removes it.

What Data Is Stored

Your plants, care schedules, care history, growth measurements, and notes are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export a backup file yourself.

Photos & Camera

If you add a photo to a plant or a journal entry, the app accesses your camera or photo library only at the moment you choose an image. Photos you select are copied into the app's local storage on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

Care reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server, and you can decline or revoke the notification permission at any time in your system settings.

Backups

Leaflet lets you export your plant journal to a backup file and restore it later. These backup files are created and stored only where you choose to save them. We never receive, access, or transmit them.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Firebase Crashlytics may collect anonymized crash reports to help us improve app stability. No personal data, plant data, or photos are included in these reports. We do not use any analytics SDK that tracks your behavior.

Ads

The free tier shows ads from Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) location, and ad interaction data, and shares it with Google's advertising partners to select and measure the ads you see, including personalised ads. This is the only information Leaflet sends off your device — your plants, photos, notes and care history never leave it. You are asked first. On iOS the system tracking prompt appears; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, you still see ads but they are not personalised. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can change the ad consent choice at any time from the app's Settings, and the iOS tracking choice under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Upgrade to Premium to remove all ads, and with them this collection entirely.

In-App Purchases

The one-time Premium purchase is processed through Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We do not handle or store any payment data directly.

Your Rights

All your data is yours. You can export it anytime from within the app, or delete it entirely by uninstalling the app. We have no central record of your data.
